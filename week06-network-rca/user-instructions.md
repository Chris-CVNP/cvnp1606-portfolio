# What To Do If You Cannot Open ACME Websites or Shared Drives

Hello Emily. Follow these steps one at a time, from top to bottom. Take your time. You cannot break anything by following them. If you have any issues following these steps or something looks different from what a step says, stop and call the helpdesk. That's what we are here for.

## Part 1: See if the internet is working

1. Look at the bottom of your screen. Find the picture you normally click to visit websites. It will be one of these three: a blue and green swirl, which is Microsoft Edge. A red, yellow, and green circle with a blue dot in the middle, which is Google Chrome. An orange fox curled around a purple ball, which is Mozilla Firefox. Click it one time.
2. A window opens. At the very top of that window there is a long white bar. Click inside that bar one time.
3. Type this exactly: www.google.com
4. Press the Enter key on your keyboard. It is the large key on the right side.
5. Wait and count slowly to 30.
6. If you see the word Google and a box to type in, your internet is working. If you see a message saying the page cannot be reached, it is not working. Write down which one you saw. Then go to Part 2.

## Part 2: Let the computer check itself

7. Look at the bottom of your screen for the four blue squares that look like a window. This is called the Start button. Click it one time.
8. A panel opens. Look for the gray symbol that looks like a gear, with the word Settings under it. Click it one time.
9. A new window opens. Look at the list of words on the left side. Click the word System.
10. Look at the right side of the window. Use the wheel on your mouse to move down the page until you see the word Troubleshoot. Click it.
11. Click the words Other troubleshooters.
12. Find the line that says Network and Internet. On the same line, at the right, there is a button that says Run. Click Run.
13. A new window opens and may ask you questions. If it asks permission to check your computer, click Yes.
14. Wait. This can take a few minutes. You do not need to do anything while it works.
15. When it says it is finished, try opening the ACME website again. If it opens, you are done. You can stop here.

## Part 3: Check one setting

16. Click the Start button again. It is the four blue squares that look like a window.
17. Click Settings again. It is the gray symbol that looks like a gear.
18. Look at the list of words on the left side. Click Network & internet.
19. Look near the top of the window. In large letters you will see either the word Ethernet or the word Wi-Fi, with the word Connected just below it. Remember which word you see.
20. Look at the list of boxes below that. Click the box that starts with the same word you just saw.
21. If the word was Wi-Fi, you will now see the name of your home internet. Click that name. If the word was Ethernet, skip this step.
22. Use the wheel on your mouse to move down the page. Look on the left for these three words: DNS server assignment. You do not need to know what they mean.
23. Look at the word written to the right of them. If it starts with the word Automatic, everything is correct. Change nothing and go to Part 4.
24. If the word is Manual, look further to the right on that same line for a button that says Edit. Click it.
25. A small window opens. Click the box at the top of it. A short list appears. Click the choice that starts with the word Automatic.
26. Click the Save button.
27. If the computer asks you for a password at any time during this part, stop. Do not type anything. Go to Part 4.
28. Close the website window by clicking the X in its top right corner. Then open it again the same way you did in step 1, and try the ACME website.

## Part 4: When to stop and call the helpdesk

Stop and call the ACME helpdesk if any one of these is true:

29. You did every step and still cannot open the ACME website or your shared drives.
30. It worked for a while and then stopped working again.
31. You see a small yellow triangle with an exclamation mark at the bottom right of your screen, near the clock.
32. The computer asked you for a password, or showed a message you did not understand. If you can, take a picture of the screen with your phone before you call.

When you call, tell us three things: your ticket number, which is CVNP1606-W06-006, that you followed these steps, and the number of the step where you stopped.

Please do not try anything else on your own. You have done everything we need, and we will take it from there.
