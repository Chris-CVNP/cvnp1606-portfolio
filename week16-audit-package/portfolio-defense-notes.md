Walk me through the most complex ticket you resolved this semester.
Week 8, CVNP1606-W08-008 was the toughest ticket. The point-of-sale workstation, ACME-W11-POS01, was constantly shutting off without shutting down cleanly. My first Event Viewer filter over the past seven days returned nothing. Therefore, I filtered over all time instead of just the past 7 days and located the events. The guide's Get-WinEvent export was blank because -MaxEvents 50 only reads the 50 latest System events before filtering; therefore, I removed -MaxEvents 50 and attempted another export. Each Kernel-Power 41 logged in the Event Viewer was paired with its corresponding 6008 unexpected shutdown event. Those were then entered into an event-timeline.md, and I completed a root cause analysis that determined it was a power-loss issue rather than a software failure. As a result, we now have a timeline of information that every claim can be cross-checked against.
What is the difference between endpoint security policies and security baselines in Intune?
A security baseline is a group of recommended settings published by Microsoft that cover many areas of concern at the same time, including Defender, BitLocker, firewalls, account lockouts, etc., and Intune reports a compliance percentage based on the entire bundle. A policy for a single area of concern, for example Antivirus, Firewall, or Disk Encryption, is referred to as an endpoint security policy. It only reports whether that particular policy has been applied to each device. During Week 14, I documented a policy called ACME-Firewall-Baseline; however, that is actually an endpoint security Firewall policy, not a security baseline. A baseline is used as the starting point, and a targeted policy is used for a certain setting.

To demonstrate compliance, I would compare the local state and policy intent side by side. Describe a time you had to choose between fixing something quickly and doing it correctly.
During Week 8 the Out-File table format truncated Event ID 6008 down to 08 in event-export.txt. A quick fix would be to modify the export command but since that was specifically what the assignment instructed us to do, I kept the export command as written and later verified the full 6008 Event IDs & messages using Get-WinEvent | Format-List and checking the Event Viewer detail pane. Both methods provided the required evidence matching the assignments and our timeline remained intact.
What would you do in the first 30 days of a help desk role at an MSP?
In the first week I would familiarize myself with the ticketing system utilized by the company, familiarize myself with documentation standards and become familiar with each client's escalation contact information. During the next two weeks I would shadow senior technicians to learn how they handle tickets and write up every ticket in accordance with the following format; Problem, Evidence, Action & Outcome. This is similar to what I accomplished during Week 9 with the four ticket triage. Prior to Day 30 I would perform a health check utilizing health-report.ps1 on all endpoints I am responsible for supporting.
